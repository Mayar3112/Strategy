I turn corporate strategy into delivered results. For 16 years I have worked across FMCG, manufacturing, power and utilities, and construction, and I currently lead enterprise strategy and projects at Mayar Foods in Saudi Arabia.

What I do

My work sits where strategy, governance and delivery meet:

  • Enterprise strategy: building and refreshing corporate strategy, with a full revamp for 2027 guided by the IASP Body of Knowledge.
  • PMO and governance: running Steering Committee and Executive Committee reporting, KPI-led performance management and governance aligned with the Saudi Companies Law.
  • Programme delivery: leading the Oracle Fusion ERP programme across three delivery teams in a hybrid Agile model, reporting to the CEO and the Strategy and Transformation Director.
  • Operational rollout: multi-branch system rollouts, business continuity exercises and compliance programmes including PDPL.

Credentials

I hold the PMP, PMI-ACP, RMP and SMP. The SMP (IASP Level 2) is one of the rarest strategy credentials, with about 120 holders worldwide and 26 in Saudi Arabia. I passed with 88%.
