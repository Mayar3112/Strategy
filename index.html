About Me

I turn corporate strategy into delivered results, and I make sure the risk, governance and compliance around it are as strong as the plan itself.

I am Abdul, an enterprise strategy and projects leader with 16 years of experience across FMCG, manufacturing, power and utilities, and construction. I am an engineer by background, so I value clear structures, measurable targets and plans that can actually be delivered. Today I lead enterprise strategy and projects at Mayar Foods in Saudi Arabia, connecting the boardroom with day-to-day operations.

Strategy

Building and refreshing corporate strategy, including a full revamp for 2027 guided by the IASP Body of Knowledge.

PMO and Delivery

Running the PMO and reporting to Steering Committees and the Executive Committee. I am leading the implementation of the new I-Sell POS system alongside the Oracle Fusion ERP programme, with KPI-led performance management throughout.

Corporate Risk

Working with stakeholders across the business to identify corporate risks, then analysing, mitigating and re-evaluating them through a structured risk framework. I report progress to the Audit Review Committee (ARC).

Governance

Developing the governance framework, the Board and its committees in line with the Saudi Companies Law.

Business Continuity

Designing business continuity plans and exercises aligned with the standards of the Business Continuity Institute (BCI).

Data Privacy Compliance

Driving the organisation's compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) under the SDAIA framework.

I hold the PMP, PMI-ACP, RMP and SMP. The SMP (IASP Level 2) is one of the rarest strategy credentials in the world, with about 120 holders worldwide and 26 in Saudi Arabia, and I passed with 88%.
