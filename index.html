Who is Abdul Wahab

From strategy to results

My work follows one thread: set the direction, turn it into projects, deliver them, and protect what has been delivered.

Set the direction

Strategy

Every year I lead the strategy cycle at Mayar Foods. It starts with the annual strategy review and the annual Catchball session, where strategic themes and objectives are challenged and refined with leadership before they are finalised.

I then lead KATA sessions that cascade the strategy through the organisation, so every function understands its part in it. I am also leading the full strategy revamp for 2027, guided by the IASP Body of Knowledge.

Turn it into projects

Corporate Projects and Innovation

Strategy only matters when it becomes work. Corporate projects emerge directly from the annual strategy cascade, and I manage them from approval to completion.

I also lead new product development (NPD) projects, working side by side with the innovation team from idea generation through execution and monitoring, using a well-developed Stage-Gate NPD framework that keeps every idea disciplined and accountable at each gate.

Deliver it

PMO and Transformation

I run the PMO, including Steering Committee and Executive Committee reporting and KPI-led performance management.

I am currently leading the implementation of the new I-Sell POS system across the organisation, alongside the Oracle Fusion ERP programme, in a hybrid Agile model.

Protect it

Risk, Governance and Compliance

Corporate risk. I work with stakeholders across the business to identify corporate risks, analyse them, agree mitigation, re-evaluate them, and report progress to the Audit Review Committee (ARC).

Governance. I develop the governance framework, the Board and its committees in line with the Saudi Companies Law.

Continuity and compliance. I build business continuity plans aligned with the standards of the Business Continuity Institute (BCI), and I drive compliance with the SDAIA Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
