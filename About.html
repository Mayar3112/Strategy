Areas of Expertise

Strategy

Strategy that cascades.

Builds the corporate strategy and translates it into clear goals for eight departments, including sales, marketing, commercial, and finance. Strategic KPIs are tracked at C-suite level, and each department’s KPI set is reviewed every quarter, so strategy stays a working plan and not a once-a-year document.

Delivery

Delivery that lands.

Directs the enterprise PMO across three streams. New product development runs from first idea to launch through a stage-gate model, with evaluation after launch. Corporate projects come directly out of the strategy cascade. IT transformation includes the co-leadership of the I-Sell point-of-sale implementation.

Resilience

Resilience that is tested.

Leads enterprise risk through a structured mitigation framework and develops business continuity policies and standards. Every year, two exercises, one tabletop and one full scale, put the organization’s resilience to the test and close the gaps they reveal. PDPL compliance is being implemented in line with SDAIA guidelines.

Governance

Governance that holds.

Develops the governance framework and committee charters under the Saudi Companies Law, and manages the committee cycle. Coordinates the Executive Committee and serves on the Strategy Committee and the Audit Review Committee.
